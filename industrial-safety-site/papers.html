Part A — 3-Mark Questions (Model Paper)

10 questions
M13Differentiate unsafe act and unsafe condition with suitable examples.
→ M1-1 (Definitions)
Unsafe acta human action against safe procedure (e.g. bypassing a machine guard, running in a plant).
Unsafe conditiona hazardous state in the workplace (e.g. frayed cable, oil spill, poor lighting).
NoteAct is behaviour; condition is the environment. Both cause accidents; control both.
M13Discuss the significance of a safety committee in improving safety performance.
→ M1-1 (Safety org)
AnswerA joint worker–management safety committee improves communication, shares ownership of safety, surfaces hazards early, speeds corrective action, and raises morale. Its very existence signals management commitment, which itself improves performance.
M23Which are the different types of permit? Highlight its suitability.
→ M2-1 (Permit)
Hot work permitfor welding/cutting/grinding in hazardous areas (needs fire-watch, gas check).
Cold work permitfor non-ignition high-risk work (confined space, electrical, lifting).
NotePermits ensure hazard assessment + controls before work.
M23Which are the five 'S' used in housekeeping?
→ M2-1 (5S)
AnswerSort (Seiri), Set in order (Seiton), Shine (Seiso), Standardize (Seiketsu), Sustain (Shitsuke). A disciplined workplace organization method that reduces hazards.
M33List the various safety features of ladders.
→ M3-1 (Ladders)
AnswerSound material; correct angle (~75°); feet secured; top secured or extends 1 m above landing; no damaged rungs; 3-point contact; never over-reached; regularly inspected.
M33How can safety of workers be ensured during demolition operations?
→ M3-1 (Demolition)
AnswerPlan + permit; sequenced top-down demolition; establish exclusion zone; isolate services (elec/gas/water); protect public/adjacent structures; trained crew + PPE; dust/noise control; appoint supervisor.
M43State the principles used in machine guarding.
→ M4-1 (Guarding)
AnswerPrevent contact with hazard; secure/substantial guards; protect from ejected parts; create no new hazard; allow safe maintenance (interlocks/LOTO). Focus on the point-of-operation.
M43State the issues in mechanical material handling.
→ M4-1 (Handling)
AnswerOverload beyond rated capacity; poor sling/rigging; insecure loads; untrained operators; inadequate maintenance of wire ropes/chains/hooks; no exclusion zone; wrong equipment selection.
M53Classify fire and state the extinguisher for a Class D (metal) fire.
→ M5-1 (Fire)
AnswerClasses: A (solids), B (liquids), C (gases), D (metals), E (electrical), F (cooking oil). Class D (Mg/Na/Al) needs a special Class-D dry powder extinguisher — water makes metal fires worse.
M53What information does an MSDS provide?
→ M5-1 (MSDS)
AnswerIdentity/hazards/composition; first-aid; firefighting; spill response; handling/storage; exposure limits; PPE; physical properties; reactivity; toxicology; disposal/transport/regulatory info.

Course-Outcome (Long) Questions

10 questions
M114Which are the various accident causation theories? Explain.
→ M1-1 (Theories)
AnswerHeinrich Domino (chain: ancestry→fault→unsafe act/condition→accident→injury; remove one domino), Bird's Loss Causation (management control failure at root), Energy Damage (uncontrolled energy release), Human Factors/Ergonomic, Systems/Swiss-Cheese, Epidemiological. All show accidents are caused and preventable.
M15Define: Accident, Reportable accident, Dangerous occurrence.
→ M1-1 (Definitions)
Accidentunplanned event causing injury and/or damage.
Reportable accidentone the law requires reporting (type/severity threshold).
Dangerous occurrencea near-miss/specified event that could cause serious harm even if none did.
M27Discuss the different types of personal protective equipment.
→ M2-1 (PPE)
Non-respiratoryhead (helmet), eye/face (goggles/shield), hearing (plugs/muffs), hand (gloves), foot (safety shoes), body (apron/coverall/high-vis).
Respiratoryair-purifying (filters, adequate O₂ only) and air-supplied/SCBA (for deficient atmospheres).
NoteMust meet standards; last line of defence.
M27How do you compare the safety performance of two industries?
→ M2-1 (Rates)
AnswerUse frequency rate (injuries per man-hours), severity rate (days lost per man-hours), incidence rate, and activity rate. Compare both frequency and severity — one industry may have few but severe events. Lower FR + SR = better.
M25Discuss the significance of the work permit system in accident prevention.
→ M2-1 (Permit)
AnswerA permit authorises and controls high-risk jobs: hazards assessed, isolations/LOTO verified, fire-watch/PPE defined, responsibilities signed. It prevents unauthorized/unsafe work and ensures controls before start — a core preventive tool.
M37Distinguish ladders and scaffolds along with their safety features.
→ M3-1 (Ladder/Scaffold)
Ladderportable, short access; angle 75°, secured, 3-point contact, inspected.
Scaffoldworking platform for sustained work; competent erection, base plates, tied, guarded, load-rated, tag system.
NoteLadder = temporary access; scaffold = working platform.
M37Discuss the safety requirements for confined space entry.
→ M2-1 (Confined space)
AnswerAtmosphere test (O₂/flammable/toxic) before & during; ventilate/purge; isolate energy (LOTO) + connected lines; permit + stand-by attendant + rescue plan; breathing apparatus if needed; communication. Never assume safe — test, ventilate, permit, watch.
M37Explain important provisions in the National Building Code for construction safety.
→ M3-1 (NBC)
AnswerNBC + relevant IS codes cover scaffolding, excavation, demolition, worker welfare, and site management (Building & Other Construction Workers Act). Provisions set minimum protective measures, competency, and statutory safety duties on site.
M414Explain the various principles used in machine guarding.
→ M4-1 (Guarding)
NoteFocus on point-of-operation.
Principlesprevent contact; secure guards; protect from ejection; no new hazard; safe maintenance (interlocks/LOTO).
Guardsfixed, interlocked, adjustable, distance.
Devicespresence-sensing (light curtain/mat), two-hand control, restraints/pull-backs, E-stop.
Exampleslathe chuck guard, grinder wheel guard.
M47Explain the issues in mechanical material handling.
→ M4-1 (Handling)
AnswerClassification (manual/mechanical); manual: assess load/task (TILE), lift with bent knees, keep load close, team-lift, palletize safely. Mechanical: trained operators, never exceed rated capacity, signaling, exclusion zones; maintain wire ropes (broken wires), chains (wear), slings (angle), hooks (latches).
M57Select different fire extinguishers according to type of fire.
→ M5-1 (Fire)
AnswerA → water/foam/ABC; B → foam/CO₂/powder; C → dry powder/CO₂; D → Class-D powder; E → CO₂/powder (cut power); F → wet chemical. Match to fuel; never water on B/D/electrical.
M514Conduct a HAZOP study for a batch reactor (methodology).
→ M5-1 (HAZOP)
AnswerDivide into nodes (e.g. feed, reactor, cooling). For a parameter (flow) apply guide-words: No flow (pump fail → no reaction, overheat), More pressure (blocked outlet → rupture → safeguard: PSV), More temperature (loss of cooling → runaway → interlock), Reverse flow (back-flow → contamination → check valve). For each: cause → consequence → existing safeguard → recommendation. Document actions/owners.
M57Determine different types of chemical hazards associated with industries.
→ M5-1 (Chem hazards)
AnswerFlammability, toxicity, reactivity, corrosivity. Controlled via substitution, containment, ventilation, PPE, procedure — informed by the MSDS. Examples: solvents (flammable/toxic), acids (corrosive), oxidizers (reactive).
