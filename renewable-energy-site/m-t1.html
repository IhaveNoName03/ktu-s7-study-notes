Diagrams to Practise

"neat diagram" questions

Solar thermal electric plant

Collectors → heat-transfer fluid → boiler/steam → turbo-generator → grid; storage tank. Label collector, pump, turbine, generator, condenser.

Flat-plate collector

Glass cover → absorber plate (black) → fluid tubes → insulation → box. Label all 5 components.

Solar cell

p-type bulk, n-type top layer, front grid (−), back contact (+), depletion zone. Show photon → e⁻/hole pair.

I–V characteristic

Curve with Voc (x-intercept), Isc (y-intercept), Pm = max rectangle; show FF.

OTEC (Anderson/closed)

Warm surface water → evaporator (refrigerant) → turbine → generator → condenser (cold deep water) → pump back.

Hybrid OTEC

Vacuum flash of seawater + separate ammonia loop; show freshwater output.

Double-basin tidal plant

Sea — dam — upper basin — dam — lower basin; turbines between basins; sluices.

Tidal range / spring-neap

Show Moon/Sun/Earth alignment for spring (max) and perpendicular for neap (min).

How to Answer by Marks

Strategy
MarksExpected structure
3-markerDefinition + 1–2 key points (or a short comparison row). ~3–4 lines.
5-markerDefinition + principle + one example/diagram label. ~8–10 lines.
10-markerFull working: principle → components → comparison table → advantages/limitations. Include a diagram.
14-markerEverything: labelled diagram, step-by-step working, formulae with substitution, both sides (advantages + limitations), real examples (Indian sites, plants).
Recurring "compare" questions: conventional vs non-conventional; flat-plate vs concentrator; stand-alone vs grid-connected PV; open vs closed vs hybrid OTEC; single vs double basin tidal. Have a two-column table ready for each.
